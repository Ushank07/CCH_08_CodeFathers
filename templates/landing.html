The problem

Citizens often do not know which government schemes they may qualify for, which conditions they fail, or which documents they still need.

The solution

Create a profile, run a transparent rule engine, then use Gemini (or a labelled fallback) to explain results and generate next steps.

The differentiator

We don’t just tell citizens what they may qualify for. We tell them why, what’s missing, and what to do next.

How it works

  1. Create a reusable profile
  2. Match against configurable scheme rules
  3. See match score, gaps, and documents
  4. Upload files and recalculate readiness
  5. Ask the AI copilot and generate letters
  6. Continue on the official portal
